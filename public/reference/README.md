## Dish photos (`public/dishes/`)

Generated by `node scripts/process-dishes.mjs` from the restaurant photos the user supplied in `images/` and the photos extracted from the PDF carta into `images/menu-pdf/` (originals unchanged). The script only applies photographic corrections: 4:3 crop at 960 × 720 (smaller sources are upscaled), tonal normalisation, then a Reinhard color transfer in CIE Lab toward the palette of the menu reference crops (`menu-hero`, `menu-entradas`, `menu-fajitas`, `menu-taquiza`) at 65% strength, light sharpening and a soft vignette. Every photo keeps its real background but the whole set shares the reference art's warm tonality.

Notes:
- `Buñuelos mexicanos.jpeg` shows the helado frito and `Helado frito.jpeg` shows the buñuelos (confirmed against the PDF descriptions), so the script swaps them.
- "Hoy pepper steak.jpeg" is the PDF's "Hot pepper steak" (output `hot-pepper-steak.webp`).
- Tamales are not in the PDF carta; they are listed without price at the owner's request.
- `Tacos de pastor.jpeg` (500 × 375) and `Tamales mexicanos.jpeg` look like stock images; confirm publication rights or replace them.

## Menu interaction

On desktop, products with choices appear first inside each category, preserving source order within each group. Mobile carousels retain source order. Missing individual photos use the original La Lupe logo in catalog, home specialties and cart. Add updates quantities without opening or scrolling: the fixed cart-count button opens a native modal over the unchanged page. Close, Escape or “Agregar más productos” return to the same position. Orders remain manual through WhatsApp; no online payments.

The cart icon stays upper-right on tablet/desktop and above WhatsApp on phones; its dialog opens against the right edge. Compact product cards keep equal dimensions; their grids and incomplete final rows are centered without stretching. Their full-width photo frames preserve the complete image with object-fit contain; no option tables remain.

Section layout: every dish uses one product card. Desktop categories use three compact columns (five Entradas appear 3+2); incomplete rows are centered. Platos estrella uses four columns on wide desktop. Beverage/cocktail listings and cart lines intentionally omit imagery. Adding a simple dish shows “Agregado al carrito con éxito” for about 2.6 seconds without moving or opening the cart.

Desktop cards share full-width 4:3 photo frames with object-fit contain, preserving complete source images rather than cropping them. A source with a wider ratio (Entrada mixta artwork) is letterboxed inside the common frame. Mobile cards retain natural image proportions. The small option thumbnails and inline price tables have been replaced by ordinary product cards.

## Reference header and supplied leaves
